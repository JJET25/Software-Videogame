HOW TO PLAY

Master the cards. Conquer the dimensions.

What is Dimension Deck?

Dimension Deck is a roguelite card battler where every run is different. You are thrown into a procedurally generated map of rooms, fighting your way through two dimensions — the Old West and the Dark Ages. Defeat enemies, collect credits, build your deck, and take down the bosses of each dimension to achieve victory. Die and everything resets — so make every decision count.

Real-Time Combat

Move freely and aim with your mouse. Cards are your weapons — time them well.

Deck Building

Start with a basic deck and upgrade it through chests, shops, and shrines across the run.

Roguelite Runs

Branching maps regenerate every run. No two runs are identical.

Objective

01

Explore Both Dimensions

Each run randomly orders the two dimensions. Navigate through rooms, clearing combat encounters to progress.

02

Defeat the Mini-Boss

At the end of the first phase of each dimension you will face a powerful mini-boss. Defeating it unlocks the path to the Final Boss.

03

Defeat the Final Boss

The ultimate guardian of each dimension. A multi-phase encounter that escalates in speed and aggression. Kill it to move to the next dimension.

04

Achieve Victory

Clear both dimensions and their final bosses to win the run and earn your place in the stats board.

Reading the HUD

HP

Health Bar

Top-left. Green fill depletes as you take damage. Reach 0 and the run ends.

SHIELD

Shield Bar

Blue bar shown above the HP bar when active. Shield absorbs incoming damage before your HP is affected.

DASH

Dash Indicator

Shows DASH READY in cyan when available, or a countdown timer during cooldown (0.8 s).

CR

Credits

Currency dropped by enemies. Spent in shop rooms to buy and upgrade cards.

CARDS

Active Card Slots

Bottom-center row (slots 1–5). The selected slot is highlighted in white. A dark overlay and timer appear when a card is on cooldown.

AUTO

Automatic Card Slots

Smaller purple-bordered slots below the active row. These cards trigger on game events automatically — no input needed.

MAP

Minimap

Shows the room graph for the current phase. Your current position is highlighted so you can plan your path to the boss.

Room Types

Start Room

Your entry point for each phase. No enemies. Use it to review your deck before pushing forward.

Combat Room

Enemies spawn when you enter. Doors remain locked until every enemy is defeated — then they open and you earn credits.

Shop Room

Spend credits to buy new cards, sell cards you no longer need, and unlock additional active or automatic card slots.

Chest Room

A free card reward. Interact with the chest to receive a random card — no credits required.

Shrine Room

Contains a dimensional portal. Interacting with it can trigger special events or advance you to the next phase.

Boss Room

The final challenge of each phase. Prepare your deck and health before entering. There is no going back once the doors seal.

Cards

Cards are your only weapons. Your deck has two rows: Active cards that you trigger manually, and Automatic cards that fire on their own when specific events occur.

Active Cards

Select with 1–5, fire with Left Click. Each has an individual cooldown displayed as a dark overlay on the slot.

  • Melee
    Melee Attacks

    Deal damage to enemies within a cone or radius around you, aimed toward your mouse cursor. Examples: Quick Strike, Iron Fist, Nova Burst, Shadow Blade.

  • Heal
    Heal Cards

    Instantly restore HP. Use them between bursts of enemy fire, not at full health. Examples: Heal Pulse, Mending Wave, Phoenix Elixir.

  • Defense
    Defense Cards

    Apply a shield that absorbs incoming damage before your HP is affected. Some grant a brief invincibility window. Examples: Wood Shield, Stone Wall, Mirror Guard, Diamond Fortress.

  • Drain
    Drain Cards

    Deal damage to a nearby enemy and convert part of it into healing. Best used offensively when low on health. Example: Blood Siphon.

Automatic Cards

Equipped in the AUTO row. They fire on their own with no input — no cooldown either. Build synergies around your playstyle.

  • On Kill
    Lifetap / Chain Kill

    Trigger when you kill an enemy. Lifetap heals 20 HP per kill. Chain Kill deals 25 damage to all nearby survivors.

  • On Hit
    Iron Skin

    Each time your card hits an enemy, you gain 8 shield. Stack hits to build an absorb buffer quickly.

  • On Damage
    Rebound / Last Stand

    Rebound punishes enemies that hit you with 15 AoE damage. Last Stand triggers 2 s of invincibility when hit below 30% HP.

  • On Dash
    Berserker Rush

    Every dash deals 20 damage to enemies within 32 px. Pair with high-frequency dashing to deal consistent chip damage.

Card Rarities

Common Rare Epic Legendary

Higher rarity cards have stronger effects but also higher shop costs and longer cooldowns. Balance your deck across rarities rather than stacking only legendaries.

Enemies

Old West

SWARM

Desert Rat

  • Small, very fast (fastest enemy type)
  • Low HP — easy to kill
  • Orbits around you, periodically dive-attacks directly at you
  • Dangerous in groups — use AoE melee cards
RANGED

Bandit

  • Medium speed, low HP
  • Keeps preferred distance from you (~180 px)
  • Strafes and fires projectiles every 1.5 s
  • Chase it down or use a wide-spread melee card
TANK

Cactus Thug

  • Slow, high HP (90), large hitbox
  • Periodically performs a fast dash directly at you
  • Contact damage is high — don't let it reach you
  • Use dash invincibility to dodge its charge

Dark Ages

SWARM

Dungeon Rat / Slime

  • Small, very fast — same behavior as Desert Rat
  • Orbits and dive-attacks in bursts
  • Usually appear in large groups
  • Nova Burst (360° melee) excels here
TANK

Skeleton

  • Slow, high HP (90), large hitbox
  • Dashes every ~2.5 s with 3× speed burst
  • Same dangerous contact damage as Cactus Thug
  • Use ranged-style spacing — dash away from charge
WARNING All enemies deal contact damage. Getting surrounded is lethal fast. Keep moving, use dash liberally, and never stand still in a full room.

Bosses

Mini-Boss 2 500 HP

Each dimension has a themed mini-boss (e.g. Skeleton King in Dark Ages). It uses the following attack pattern, escalating across 3 phases:

PhaseTriggerNew Behavior
Phase 1 Start Moves toward you, fires a 3-bullet spread burst, summons Swarm enemies every 7 s
Phase 2 ≤ 65% HP Faster movement and dash, adds a 12-bullet radial ring attack, summons Ranged enemies
Phase 3 ≤ 30% HP Becomes enraged — faster burst fire (every 0.8 s), 18-bullet radial ring, summons Tanks and Swarm

Final Boss 5 000 HP

The true guardian of each dimension. Significantly harder than the mini-boss with an added laser attack in phase 2+:

PhaseTriggerNew Behavior
Phase 1 Start 3-bullet burst (every 0.7 s), 18-bullet radial ring, dash when player within ~420 px, summons Swarm
Phase 2 ≤ 70% HP Higher speed and dash speed, fires a concentrated laser burst every 2 s, summons Ranged enemies
Phase 3 ≤ 30% HP Extreme speed — burst every 0.35 s with 5 bullets, 30-bullet radial ring every 1.2 s, laser every 1 s, summons Tank enemies
BOSS TIP Save your heal cards and defense cards for phase transitions. Both bosses gain burst speed boosts as HP drops — the first few seconds of a new phase are the most dangerous.

Pro Tips

01

Dash Through Bullets

Your dash grants invincibility for its full 0.15 s. You can dash directly into a bullet spray and take zero damage if timed correctly.

02

Clear Rooms for Credits

You only earn credits by fully clearing combat rooms. Rushing past enemies starves you of shop currency — clear every room on the path.

03

Mix Card Types

A pure-damage deck dies to burst damage. A pure-heal deck can't kill fast enough. One heal, one defense, two attacks, and one drain card is a reliable starting balance.

04

Use the Deck Manager Between Rooms

Press Tab before entering a tough room to rearrange cards. Put your fastest-cooldown card in slot 1 so it's always the default selection.

05

Prioritize Slot Upgrades

Unlocking a 4th active card slot or extra auto slots is often worth more than a single new card. More slots = more simultaneous effects.

06

Swarm Rooms Need AoE

Swarm enemies orbit fast. Single-target cards struggle against five dive-bombing rats. Always keep at least one card with wide spread (≥ 180°) in your deck.
